Space Wrecker - Privacy Policy

Developer: Animaster · App: Space Wrecker (Android, package com.animaster.spacewrecker) · Effective date: 8 September 2026

1. Summary

Space Wrecker does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs): no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no social features. It does not require an account, does not request any device permissions beyond vibration, and does not connect to the internet.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (current level, coins, win streak, unlocked blocks) and your settings (sound, vibration) are saved only on your own device so the game can resume where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

3. Information we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data or diagnostics.

4. Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it can be played by users of any age without any personal information being processed.

5. Future changes

If a future version of the game adds analytics or advertising SDKs, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe exactly what is collected, by whom and why, and the effective date above will change. The current version of the policy is always published at this address.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play store listing.
