Space Wrecker - Privacy Policy

Developer: Animaster Studio · App: Space Wrecker (Android, package com.animaster.spacewrecker) · Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Space Wrecker is made by Animaster Studio. If you have any question about this policy or your data, email us at mdakibnur18032@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

When you play Space Wrecker, the game and the services built into it collect:

  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address (country or region level).
  • Device and advertising IDs, such as your device's advertising ID and technical details like the device model and operating system version.
  • App interactions: the levels you play, wins and losses, and how long you play.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics, so we can find and fix problems.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and the game does not ask you to create an account.

3. Why we collect it

  • Analytics: to understand how the game is played and make it better.
  • Advertising and marketing: to measure how well our ad campaigns bring new players to the game.

4. Who else receives it

The data above is collected through these third-party services. Each one handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Yso Corp GameUtils (analytics) – privacy policy
  • Meta / Facebook SDK – privacy policy
  • AppLovin MAX – privacy policy
  • Tenjin – privacy policy

We do not sell your data.

5. How it is protected

All data is sent over a secure, encrypted HTTPS connection.

6. Delete your data

You can ask us to delete the data collected about you at any time:

  • Email mdakibnur18032@gmail.com with the subject "Space Wrecker data deletion".
  • Include your device's advertising ID so we can find your data. On Android you can see it under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

We delete the data within 30 days of your request. You can also reset your advertising ID at any time in the same Ads settings screen, which disconnects future data from the old ID.

7. Children

Space Wrecker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects or why, we will update this page and the date at the top before releasing that version. The current policy is always published at this address.
